Ask about your setup ↗
Do I have to replace my existing business number?+

Not necessarily. Depending on your carrier and phone setup, call forwarding or another supported routing method may let you keep your current number. We'll review the options before activation.

Will the AI book appointments automatically?+

It can capture appointment requests. Automatic calendar booking depends on the calendar integration and workflow being available, configured and tested for your business.

Can callers reach a real person?+

We can configure human handoff or callback rules where your phone system and selected service support them. We'll agree on those rules with you before launch.

How do you set it up for my business?+

You complete the discovery brief, we prepare a tailored demo, and you review it. After you approve the written plan and costs, we coordinate configuration and testing.

How is the setup fee billed?+

A one-time setup fee of $99 is invoiced separately by Solomon Jarman, sole proprietor. It is separate from your recurring monthly plan and is not collected through this website. We confirm the amount and invoice details with you before setup begins.

Is there a long-term contract or payment on this page?+

This page only collects your discovery information. It does not take payment or activate a subscription. Contract terms and monthly billing are confirmed separately before service begins.
