Internal provider-cost reference — admin only

Use these as cost inputs, not customer-facing marketing. Verify the actual account invoice and exact configuration before quoting. Pass third-party charges through at cost; show any AVR labor or management fee as a separate line.

  • SMS usage: $0.01 per message, plus a $20/month SMS subscription per enabled number (where applicable).
  • US SMS registration: business profile approval, brand registration (commonly $4 low-volume or $45 standard) and campaign registration (commonly $15). A recurring $20/month SMS subscription per number and $0.01/message apply on the managed SMS route; other campaign, carrier and pass-through fees may apply.
  • Concurrency is shared at workspace level: first 20 simultaneous active calls included; $8/month for each additional slot. Burst usage, if enabled, can add $0.10/min for calls above the limit.
  • Additional phone number: $2/month per provider-managed number.
  • Knowledge base: first 10 free; then $8/month per additional knowledge base, plus $0.005/min processing where applicable.
  • Advanced denoising: +$0.005/min; safety guardrails: +$0.005/min; personal-information redaction: +$0.01/min.
  • Voice, language model, telephony country, branded calling, verification and other features vary by selected configuration.

Reference: provider pricing · SMS and phone registration requirements. Recheck before each quote.
