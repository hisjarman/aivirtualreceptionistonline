Privacy notice · Effective November 5, 2026

Your information.
Your choice.

This notice explains how AI Virtual Receptionist (“we”, “us”) handles information submitted through aivirtualreceptionist.online, including the business discovery form and AI Trust Lab.

Contact permission is separate. Each form asks whether you agree to be contacted. Choosing “No” does not block a Trust Lab submission. We do not treat submission of a form as permission for unrelated marketing.

1. Information collected

  • Business discovery form: name, email address, phone number, business name, industry, website, service area, call volume, business hours, services, call-handling requirements, FAQs, phone setup, selected plan, notes, contact preference and submission time.
  • AI Trust Lab: your Clerk account identifier for sign-in and account security; submission title and description; industry; display name; stress-test difficulty score where provided; contact preference; submission time; and a one-way hashed IP address used to help prevent spam and abuse. The verified email is copied into the Trust Lab submission record only when you select Yes to contact permission.
  • Technical information: our hosting, authentication, database and security providers may process IP address, browser/device information, request logs and other technical data needed to deliver and protect the service.

2. How we use information

We use business inquiry information to understand your needs, review the request and prepare the demo or proposal you asked for. We use Trust Lab information to authenticate accounts, receive and moderate tests, prevent abuse, administer the community feed and improve receptionist configurations and testing. We may use aggregated or de-identified counts to display dashboard statistics.

3. Contact choices

On the business form, “Yes” allows us to contact you about that inquiry; “No” means we will not follow up. On the Trust Lab form, “Yes” allows us to email you about that submission; “No” means we will not contact you about it. Contact permission is not consent to unrelated promotional messages. You can change your preference or withdraw permission by contacting us through the business inquiry form and explaining your request; withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out.

4. What is public

Trust Lab submissions are private while pending. A human moderator must approve a submission before publication. If approved, the submission title, description, industry, display name and submission date may be shown on the public Trust Lab page and dashboard. Your account email, Clerk identifier, contact preference, hashed IP address and moderation/security records are not part of the public feed. If you select Yes, your verified email is stored with the submission and visible only to authorized moderators for follow-up about that submission; if you select No, your email is not saved in the Trust Lab submission record. Use “Anonymous” or a pseudonym as your display name, and do not put personal, customer, payment, credential or confidential business information in a submission.

5. Service providers

We use third-party providers to operate the website and forms. The current implementation uses GitHub Pages for website hosting, Formspree to receive and forward business discovery form submissions, Clerk for Trust Lab account authentication, and Cloudflare Workers and D1 for Trust Lab API processing and storage. The site also loads Google Fonts. These providers process information as needed to provide their services and under their own terms and privacy practices. Embedded chat functionality, when used, may process the messages and technical information submitted through that widget.

6. Sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information. We do not disclose private Trust Lab account details or private business inquiry submissions publicly. Information may be processed by the service providers listed above, disclosed when legally required, or used to protect the website, users and service.

7. Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to handle the inquiry, administer accounts and submissions, maintain moderation and security records, and meet legal obligations. Retention periods may differ by provider. We use access controls, authentication, limited public fields and hashed IP identifiers in the Trust Lab, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not submit sensitive information.

8. Your choices and requests

You may ask us to access, correct or delete information you submitted, or withdraw contact permission, subject to legal, security and operational requirements. Use the business inquiry form and clearly identify the submission or account concerned. You may also manage your Clerk account through its account controls. Deleting an account does not automatically remove information already approved and published; ask us to review that separately.

9. Children

This website and the Trust Lab are intended for business users and are not directed to children. Do not submit information about children.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this notice when the site or its providers change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made. For a privacy request, use the business inquiry form and select “No” for contact permission if you do not want a response beyond what is necessary to process the privacy request.
