Custom Voice Cloning

Give your AI receptionist a voice that sounds like you, your team member, or your brand spokesperson.

What we'll need from you
  • A clean voice recording: ideally 2–5 minutes of one person speaking naturally, with no music, interruptions, or background noise.
  • A sample script: provide a few paragraphs to read, or use a script we'll supply. Include the words, names, and phrases your receptionist may say.
  • Permission to use the voice: you must be the speaker or have the speaker's explicit authorization to create and use the voice clone.
  • Recording format: a clear audio file. We'll confirm the accepted file format and secure upload method when your request is approved.

Please don't upload sensitive or private conversations. A purpose-made recording is best. Recording and provider requirements can vary.
