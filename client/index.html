OPTIONAL PREMIUM ADD-ON

A voice that sounds like your brand.

Here is the typical process. We confirm the provider, recording requirements, availability and total cost with you before any work or charge begins.

  1. 1. Request and planTell us whose voice you want to use, the languages needed and where the receptionist will use it.
  2. 2. Permission and recordingThe speaker must be you or someone who has explicitly authorized the cloning and intended use. We will provide a purpose-made script and secure submission instructions. A clean, quiet recording is usually needed; exact length and format depend on the selected provider.
  3. 3. Provider review and voice creationWe evaluate compatible third-party options—potentially ElevenLabs, Cartesia or Resemble AI—based on voice quality, language support, commercial-use terms, consent checks and compatibility with the Retell AI call setup. These are candidates, not guaranteed integrations or preselected vendors.
  4. 4. Configure and testIf the chosen provider is supported, we connect the approved voice, test pronunciation and call quality, and let you review it before launch.
Important: Never submit someone else’s voice without their explicit permission. Do not send private conversations, passwords or sensitive personal information. Provider eligibility, verification, pricing and technical compatibility must be confirmed before proceeding.
