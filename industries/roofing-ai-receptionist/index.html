ROOFING-SPECIFIC CALL HANDLING

What happens when a homeowner calls your roofing company?

We tailor the questions and handoff instructions to your services, coverage area and operating hours. Here are examples of the information your receptionist can be configured to collect.

1. Storm damage after hail or high winds

Capture the property address, type of visible damage, when it happened, whether water is entering the home, and the best callback number. Your team can review the request and decide the next step.

2. An active roof leak

Ask whether water is entering now, which area of the property is affected, and where the property is located. The receptionist follows your approved urgent-call instructions; it does not independently dispatch emergency crews.

3. A roof replacement or inspection estimate

Collect the caller's name, contact details, property location, roof or project type, and preferred time for follow-up. Appointment booking is only offered where your selected setup supports it.

4. An after-hours roofing inquiry

Explain the next step using your approved after-hours message, capture the request, and pass the details to your team through the configured handoff.

Illustrative call example

Homeowner: "We had a storm last night, and now water is coming through the ceiling."

AI receptionist: "I'm sorry you're dealing with that. I can collect a few details so your roofing team can review the request. What's the property address?"

Homeowner: "It's 123 Oak Street."

AI receptionist: "Thank you. Is water still coming in, and what's the best number for your team to reach you?"

This is an illustrative scripted example, not a live call or emergency dispatch service.

ROOFING FAQ

Questions roofing contractors ask.

Can an AI receptionist answer calls while my crew is working?

Yes. The receptionist can answer calls routed to the configured phone number or call path. The exact routing setup depends on your phone provider and approved configuration.

Can it capture storm damage and roof leak requests?

It can be configured to ask about the property address, reported damage, whether water is entering, urgency and callback details. Your team defines the escalation instructions.

Can it collect roofing estimate requests?

Yes. It can collect contact details, property location and basic project information so your team can follow up. Specific scheduling or CRM actions depend on supported integrations.

Can it transfer urgent calls to my team?

Call routing or transfer can be configured where supported by the selected phone platform. We agree on the rules with you before launch.

Do I have to write prompts or build call flows?

No. This is a managed service. We handle the technical configuration and review the experience with you.

Can I keep my existing business phone number?

Often, calls can be forwarded from an existing number, but the available options depend on your carrier and phone system. We confirm the best approach during discovery.

What happens if the receptionist doesn't know an answer?

We configure approved responses and fallback instructions. The receptionist should not invent roofing prices, inspection availability, warranties or technical advice.
