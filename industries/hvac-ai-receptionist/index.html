HVAC-SPECIFIC CALL HANDLING

What happens when a customer calls your HVAC company?

We tailor the questions and handoff instructions to your services, service area and operating hours. These are examples of details the receptionist can be configured to collect.

1. No heat or no cooling

Ask what equipment is affected, whether the system is running, the property location and how best to reach the customer. Follow the company's approved urgent-call instructions.

2. Heating or cooling system repair

Capture system type, symptoms, service address, contact details and preferred callback window so a technician can review the request.

3. Maintenance and tune-up requests

Collect equipment and service details, location and preferred timing. Appointment booking is offered only if the configured calendar workflow supports it.

4. After-hours HVAC call

Use the approved after-hours message and escalation path. Urgent calls are transferred or notified only where the selected phone setup supports that workflow.

Illustrative call example

Caller: "Our air conditioner stopped cooling and it's 90 degrees inside."
AI receptionist: "I can collect details for your HVAC team. Is the system running at all, and what's the service address?"

Illustrative example only. It is not a live call, technical diagnosis or emergency dispatch service.

HVAC FAQ

Questions HVAC contractors ask.

Can it handle no-heat or no-cooling calls?

It can collect the reported issue, system details, service address and callback information, then follow your approved escalation instructions.

Can it book HVAC maintenance appointments?

It can capture appointment requests. Automatic booking depends on the selected calendar integration and a configured, tested workflow.

Can it route emergency HVAC calls?

Transfer or notification rules can be configured where supported by your selected phone platform. Your business defines emergency criteria and escalation contacts.

Will it diagnose an HVAC problem?

No. It collects caller-reported symptoms and uses approved information. It should not replace a qualified technician's diagnosis or invent technical advice.

Do I have to write prompts or build call flows?

No. This is a managed service. We handle the technical configuration and review the experience with you.

Can I keep my existing business phone number?

Often, calls can be forwarded from an existing number, but options depend on your carrier and phone system. We confirm the best approach during discovery.

What happens if the receptionist does not know an answer?

We configure approved responses and fallback instructions. It should not invent prices, availability, warranties or technical advice.
