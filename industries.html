BUILT AROUND THE WAY YOUR INDUSTRY WORKS

Not just an AI voice.
A business-specific playbook.

We configure each receptionist around your services, approved answers, caller needs and handoff rules. The examples below show the starting point—not a one-size-fits-all script.

01 / HOME SERVICES

Roofing, plumbing, HVAC & electrical

Configured for: service-area checks, issue descriptions, property details, urgency, estimate requests and dispatch or on-call handoffs.

  • Ask whether there is an active leak, loss of heat/cooling, electrical hazard or other urgent condition.
  • Follow your approved emergency instructions; do not diagnose, promise arrival times or dispatch without authorization.
  • Capture the address and callback details needed by your team.
GuardrailSafety concerns trigger the approved escalation path—not improvised repair advice.
02 / CONTRACTORS & PROPERTY

General contractors, cleaning, landscaping & pest control

Configured for: project type, property size, site address, access requirements, service frequency and estimate or consultation requests.

  • Collect only the project details your team needs to qualify the request.
  • Use approved service areas, availability and preparation instructions.
  • Route hazardous materials, structural danger, infestations or unsafe-site concerns to a person.
GuardrailNo invented quotes, scope commitments, guarantees or technical safety instructions.
03 / HEALTHCARE & WELLNESS

Dental, medical, veterinary & wellness practices

Configured for: office hours, appointment requests, location, approved preparation information and staff routing.

  • Keep the agent administrative; do not diagnose, triage clinically, recommend treatment or interpret symptoms.
  • Route urgent symptoms, medication questions and clinical decisions to qualified staff or the practice's approved emergency instructions.
  • Limit sensitive information collection and activate regulated-data workflows only after privacy, security, vendor and contract review.
GuardrailNo protected health information workflows until applicable safeguards and agreements are verified; no claim of HIPAA certification.
04 / LEGAL & PROFESSIONAL

Law firms, accounting, tax & insurance

Configured for: new-inquiry intake, practice or service area, conflict-check handoff prompts, consultation requests and office routing.

  • Capture a brief contact reason without inviting unnecessary confidential or privileged details.
  • Use only firm-approved descriptions of services, fees, office policies and next steps.
  • Route legal, tax, coverage and claim-specific questions to the responsible professional.
GuardrailNo legal, tax, insurance or financial advice; no attorney-client, accountant-client or coverage relationship is implied by a call.
05 / REAL ESTATE & FINANCE

Real estate, property management & mortgage

Configured for: property or listing inquiries, viewing requests, maintenance intake, lead routing and callback scheduling.

  • Use verified listing details, approved availability and customer-authorized scripts.
  • Route financing, eligibility, valuation, legal and transaction questions to licensed staff.
  • Do not collect Social Security numbers, bank credentials or payment-card details in ordinary calls.
GuardrailNo lending decisions, financial recommendations, fair-housing screening or promises about approval, returns or property condition.
06 / AUTOMOTIVE & TRANSPORT

Auto repair, dealerships, towing & detailing

Configured for: vehicle year/make/model, requested service, warning-light description, location, appointment or tow-request details.

  • Use approved service menus and shop hours; mark estimates as estimates when authorized.
  • Route breakdowns, crashes, roadside danger and suspected safety defects to the approved human or emergency path.
  • Never tell a caller a vehicle is safe to drive or diagnose a fault from a brief description.
GuardrailNo safety assurances, mechanical diagnosis, repair guarantees or unauthorized dispatch commitments.
07 / HOSPITALITY & FOOD

Restaurants, hotels & event venues

Configured for: hours, reservation or event inquiries, party size, availability requests, directions and approved venue policies.

  • Only confirm bookings when a connected booking workflow returns a verified confirmation.
  • Use venue-approved menu and allergen information; escalate allergy and food-safety questions to staff.
  • Never promise an allergen-free meal, room, service or event arrangement without explicit verified confirmation.
GuardrailNo invented availability, reservation confirmation, allergen assurances or event guarantees.
08 / RETAIL & PERSONAL SERVICES

Salons, spas, gyms, retail & community organizations

Configured for: service menus, class or appointment requests, store hours, membership FAQs, event details and staff handoffs.

  • Answer only from approved schedules, prices, policies and product information.
  • Route complaints, cancellations, accessibility requests and sensitive situations to the right person.
  • Do not make health, safety, product-performance or membership promises that are not documented.
GuardrailNo invented discounts, refunds, product claims, medical advice or policy exceptions.
EVERY AGENT / BASELINE GUARDRAILS

Rules we apply across every industry

Industry-specific behavior sits on top of a consistent safety and quality framework.

  • Approved knowledge onlyUse the business facts, scripts, prices, hours and policies the client has approved. If information is missing, say so and offer a handoff.
  • Clear AI identityIdentify the receptionist as AI where required and follow the approved recording, transcription and messaging notices.
  • No unauthorized commitmentsDo not finalize prices, refunds, contracts, bookings, dispatches or account changes unless the configured workflow is authorized and confirms success.
  • Privacy by designAsk only for information needed for the task. Avoid passwords, payment credentials and unnecessary sensitive details.
  • Escalate uncertainty and riskWhen a caller describes an emergency, safety issue, regulated question or uncertain situation, follow the customer's approved human or emergency escalation path.
  • Test before activationWe review realistic scenarios and edge cases with the client, document approved behavior and obtain approval of the configured experience before launch.
Important: These are configuration standards and operating boundaries, not a guarantee that an AI system will never make an error or a claim of legal compliance. Specific workflows depend on the written scope, provider capabilities, integrations and customer approval. Higher-risk or regulated workflows may be restricted or declined.
