Choose the right guide for your business

If you are comparing providers, begin with the AI receptionist cost guide and the AI versus human receptionist comparison. They explain the trade-offs around usage charges, availability, customer experience and human escalation.

If you are evaluating the underlying voice technology, read our Retell AI pricing explainer. If your goal is to understand the business case, use the missed-call opportunity calculator guide and substitute your own call data rather than relying on generic industry averages.

For roofing contractors, our roofing-specific guide covers estimate intake, urgent leak reports, service areas and when a caller should be transferred to a person.
