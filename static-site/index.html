GOOD QUESTIONS

Know what you're
getting before you start.

We believe in clear expectations. Here's what to know about a managed AI receptionist.

Ask us about your setup →
Do I have to set up the AI myself?+

No. This is a managed service. We gather your business information, configure the receptionist, test agreed scenarios, and coordinate launch with you.

Can it book appointments?+

It can capture appointment requests. Confirmed calendar booking depends on the calendar integration and rules supported for your setup. We won't claim an appointment is booked unless it really is.

Can callers reach a real person?+

We define escalation and transfer rules with you. If a live transfer isn't available for a particular call path, the receptionist can capture a message and callback request.

Will it replace my staff?+

It's designed to handle routine first conversations and organize requests—not to replace your judgment or every human interaction. You decide when your team should take over.

How do I get started?+

Request a demo. We'll learn about your business, prepare a sample call experience, and send a clear proposal for your review. You approve before anything goes live or payment is requested.
